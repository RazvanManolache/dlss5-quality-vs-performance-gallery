Female and male neutral walking, idle waiting, looking around, talking/listening gestures, photography, text messaging, seated idle and presenting are retargeted from Rocketbox motion capture. Expressions use the supplied eye-blink, smile and jaw-open blend shapes, driven by procedural timings. Mouth movement is ambient animation, without dialogue or lip synchronization. The animal idle is procedural bone animation, not an animal motion-capture clip. Family routes and camera/phone props are native gallery code.

Source FBX/TGA URLs, Git blob hashes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [the expanded source manifest](licenses/Rocketbox-crowd-sources.json). The original three-person record remains in [licenses/Rocketbox-source-manifest.json](licenses/Rocketbox-source-manifest.json). Prepared derivatives preserve authored head/body maps and alpha-cutout hair. Compact bone frames and bind meshes replace baked per-vertex pose archives. Four mesh detail levels are generated with Blender body decimation; the FBXs contain only a high mesh. Original human head and hair triangles are retained at every level. These are derived levels, not authored Rocketbox LODs. No model, texture or animation asset was generated by Phoenix.

GPU skinning feeds the same current geometry to rasterization and DXR and generates previous-frame geometry for motion vectors. Protected Phoenix mode retains native clothing and hair; full-frame mode processes the whole image. Each half has an independent character body-detail selector; distance detail preserves the selected person's full mesh.

Retained Poly Haven exhibits and marble sculptures are CC0. Authors, links and original downloads are in [the exhibit credits](licenses/Exhibit-credits.md). There is no Fox model in the active scene.

The runtime also initializes the earlier Infinite head scan by Lee Perry-Smith, based on work at www.triplegangers.com, although it is not placed in the gallery. Its prepared meshes and skin maps retain the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license.
