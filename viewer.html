What changed, and what still differs from Beauty

Light + detail removes the +1.6 mip bias on textured surfaces, restores normal-map strength, and exempts visitors, vases and the horse from the broad lighting blur. The same mip chains, person geometry, alpha-tested hair and animations were already loaded; memory allocations and assets are unchanged. The optional shadow preset increases direct samples only on clothing from one to four.

Both improved presets keep Very light's geometry, zero indirect-light samples and sky reflections. Beauty still has 20 direct samples, 16 indirect samples, 24 reflection samples, extra bounce lighting and skin diffusion. These inputs preserve more authored surface detail. Lighting and overall quality are not equivalent to Beauty.

Motion clips use the same six seconds and the same real camera following the pink-shirt visitor, with a centered image region shown for inspection. More detail also exposes existing raster edges and shadow changes. This eight-frame-per-second evidence clip is a visual check, not a high-frame-rate flicker certification.
