The downloaded README is included. The source commit and file hashes are pinned in manifest.json.

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Prepared derivatives: models normalized into native Y-up coordinates, original
UVs remapped into material atlases, maps resized to 1024 pixels per material,
distant geometry decimated, and the Fox Survey skeletal cycle sampled into
90 poses. The native renderer linearly interpolates those poses and refits the
ray-tracing geometry on every frame. Camera glass is rendered opaque in this
approximate material renderer. The original downloads remain intact.

The existing Marble Bust 01 by Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven is CC0; its original
files and credits remain in ../../../scene_lab/assets. The human scan is no longer
placed in this scene. It remains intact in the earlier scene and asset archive.
